## What the run taught

- ChatGPT's four are more *painted* (brush and gouache texture, warmer paper); Gemini's are more *printed* (cleaner halftone, flatter colour). Both are period-right; the site can carry both if the printed ones are used for diagrams and spots and the painted ones for covers and scenes.
- Faces crept in on two Gemini pieces despite the negative prompt; the ChatGPT ones kept to hands. Keep "seen from behind" in the prompt, not just "no faces".
- Image 7 invented a real publisher. Add "no masthead, no publication name" to every prompt.
- None of the thirteen is "AI-looking" at thumbnail size. At full size, 8 and 9 hold up best.

## Housekeeping

The site's art goes in `public/images/art/<name>.{jpg,png}` with a `<name>.txt` beside it holding the prompt, the tool, and the date — these originals stay here as the record. Rename at copy time; do not rename these.
