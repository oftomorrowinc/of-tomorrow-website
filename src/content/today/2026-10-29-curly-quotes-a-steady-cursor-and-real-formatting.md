The team asked for curly quotes in the Writing Assistant, and they shipped the same day. A bug that made the cursor jump to the end of a passage mid-delete was found and fixed. Strikethrough, block quotes, lists and hotkeys arrived, and dashes written by the model now match the story's own style.
