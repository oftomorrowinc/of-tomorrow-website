Todd ruled the plan for a BYOLLM installer on Windows and macOS: its own Node, a six-screen setup window and a tray icon, with Linux keeping the terminal. Updates will be on by default for new setups, offered to our own machines first. Apple's company enrolment, the slow step, starts now.
